IMMER v2 — Improvised Movement+Music Ensemble

Web-driven score for an ensemble of dancer-musicians. Performers join from any phone, toggle between music and dance roles, and the Max patch tracks pair coverage and solos in real time. v2 adds an optional cloud relay (Cloudflare Worker) so remote performers can join over the internet alongside the LAN room.
